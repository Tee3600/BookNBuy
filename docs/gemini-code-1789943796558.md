# Product Requirement Document (PRD): BookNBuy

## Product Overview
BookNBuy is a two-sided, multi-vendor marketplace platform designed to bridge physical product retail with service appointments in a single unified web and mobile experience. Built specifically to handle regional market realities—such as localized logistics, informal dispatch channels, localized NGN payment ecosystems, and cross-border transactions—BookNBuy allows users to buy goods, book professional services, or list their own offerings with a single account.

---

## 1. Objectives & Goals
* **Unified Commerce:** Enable seamless cross-activity between buying goods and booking services without requiring separate account creation.
* **Trust & Security:** Reduce payment friction and fraud through automated escrow holding and a structured dispute inspection period.
* **Localized Payment & Multi-Currency Engine:** Support NGN-native payment mechanisms (virtual bank transfers, cards, USSD, mobile wallets) while allowing international diaspora buyers to pay in USD, GBP, or EUR.
* **Market-Tailored Logistics:** Accommodate formal delivery partners (couriers) as well as localized, informal delivery channels (bike dispatch, motor park waybills, and WhatsApp/SMS status updates).
* **Conversion & Retainment:** Lower cart abandonment by streamlining checkout and offering instant time-slot scheduling.

---

## 2. Target Audience & Personas

### Persona A: The Casual Buyer & Booker ("Tobi")
* **Profile:** Urban professional living in Lagos, mobile-first user.
* **Needs:** Wants to buy products (e.g., tech accessories, clothing) and book appointments (e.g., tailor, salon, home repair) from one app using instant bank transfers or debit cards.
* **Pain Points:** Unreliable fulfillment, fear of sending money before service delivery, lack of real-time communication during transit.

### Persona B: The Micro-Vendor / Service Pro ("Amaka")
* **Profile:** Runs a boutique clothing store and offers bespoke tailoring services in Abuja.
* **Needs:** A flexible dashboard to list products, control booking schedules, receive automated Naira payouts, and manage earnings.
* **Pain Points:** Double-booking issues, managing manual bank transfer proof/receipts, high operational costs with rigid logistics platforms.

### Persona C: The Diaspora Buyer ("Kemi")
* **Profile:** Nigerian living in the UK or US.
* **Needs:** Wants to pay in GBP/USD using an international card or Apple Pay to purchase products or book services for family back home in Nigeria.
* **Pain Points:** High cross-border transaction fees, merchant rejection of foreign cards, currency conversion uncertainty.

## 3. Core Product Requirements & User Stories

### 3.1 Authentication & Accounts (single account for buy/book/sell)
* As a user, I can sign up/login with email+password and OTP (SMS/WhatsApp) so I have one identity.
* As a user, I can become a vendor by completing KYC (name, phone, bank account, ID) without creating a new account.
* As a diaspora buyer, I can check out without BVN/NIN, using email + international card.

### 3.2 Product Catalog (goods)
* As a vendor, I can create/edit/archive products with title, photos, price (NGN), stock, category, location.
* As a buyer, I can search/filter by keyword, category, price, location, and see vendor rating.
* As a buyer, I can view product detail with stock status and delivery estimate.

### 3.3 Service Catalog & Booking (services)
* As a vendor, I can create services with duration, price, buffer time, working hours, and blackout dates.
* As a vendor, I can prevent double-booking via slot locking.
* As a buyer, I can see real-time availability, pick a slot, add notes, and book + pay in one flow.
* As a buyer/vendor, I can reschedule/cancel per policy (e.g., free cancel >24h, fee within 24h).

### 3.4 Cart, Checkout & Orders
* As a buyer, I can check out products and service bookings together in one cart.
* As a buyer, I can pay via Paystack/Flutterwave: card, bank transfer (virtual account), USSD, mobile wallet, Apple Pay (diaspora).
* As a buyer, I see all fees upfront: subtotal, delivery, service fee, FX quote if paying in USD/GBP/EUR.

### 3.5 Vendor Dashboard & Payouts
* As a vendor, I see orders, bookings, earnings (pending/available/paid out), and low-stock alerts.
* As a vendor, I receive automated NGN payouts to bank account after escrow release, minus commission.
* As a vendor, I can manage availability calendar and fulfillment status (pending/confirmed/in-transit/delivered/completed).

### 3.6 Trust: Escrow, Disputes, Reviews
* As a buyer, my payment is held in escrow until I confirm receipt/completion or 48-72h inspection window expires.
* As a buyer, I can raise a dispute with photos/reason; funds stay frozen until admin/vendor resolves (refund/partial/release).
* As a user, I can leave 1-5 star reviews only after completed order/booking.

### 3.7 Logistics
* Formal: assign courier, tracking ID, status updates.
* Informal (MVP-manual): vendor enters dispatch type (bike dispatch, motor park waybill with park/receipt no., self-delivery), buyer gets WhatsApp/SMS updates. No live GPS in MVP.
* As a buyer, I get notifications at confirmed, in-transit, delivered, completed.

### 3.8 Notifications
* MVP: email + in-app + WhatsApp/SMS via Termii/Africa's Talking for order/booking status, OTP, payout.

## 4. Functional Requirements (MVP)

1. Auth: email+password, session (NextAuth), roles: `BUYER`, `VENDOR`, `ADMIN` (single user can have BUYER+VENDOR).
2. Vendor onboarding: KYC form + admin approve/reject.
3. Products CRUD + image upload (Cloudinary/S3) + stock decrement on order.
4. Services CRUD + availability rules + slot generation (15/30/60 min) + booking lock (DB unique constraint on vendor+start_time).
5. Unified cart: line items type `PRODUCT` or `BOOKING`.
6. Checkout: create `Order` + `OrderItems` + `Payment` (status pending), redirect to Paystack, webhook verifies, marks paid.
7. Escrow ledger: `Transaction` states: `HELD > RELEASED | REFUNDED | PARTIAL`. Auto-release job after 72h if no dispute.
8. Bookings state machine: `PENDING_PAYMENT > CONFIRMED > COMPLETED | CANCELLED | NO_SHOW`.
9. Orders state machine: `PENDING > PAID > FULFILLING > IN_TRANSIT > DELIVERED > COMPLETED | DISPUTED | REFUNDED`.
10. Disputes: buyer opens within inspection window, admin resolves.
11. Payouts: vendor bank accounts, manual/auto NGN transfer record (Paystack Transfer in post-MVP; MVP can be manual mark-paid).
12. Reviews: one per completed order/booking.
13. Admin: user/vendor approval, orders/disputes queue, commission config, audit log.

---

## 5. Payments & Escrow Design

* Provider: Paystack (primary, best NGN virtual accounts/USSD/cards + Apple Pay for diaspora). Flutterwave as fallback for multi-currency.
* Currency: NGN as settlement currency. Display USD/GBP/EUR quote at checkout using provider FX + 2% buffer shown explicitly.
* Flow: checkout -> Paystack initialize -> webhook `charge.success` -> mark Payment PAID -> escrow HELD -> on confirm/timeout -> RELEASED to vendor payable balance.
* Commission: e.g., 8% products, 10% services + ₦100 + payment gateway fee passed through (configurable).
* Refunds: via Paystack refund API or manual bank transfer recorded in ledger.

## 6. Data Model (MVP entities)

* `User(id, name, email, phone, passwordHash, roles, kycStatus)`
* `VendorProfile(userId, businessName, city, bankCode, accountNumber, approved)`
* `Product(id, vendorId, title, description, priceNGN, stock, images, status)`
* `Service(id, vendorId, title, priceNGN, durationMin, bufferMin, workingHours JSON)`
* `Booking(id, serviceId, buyerId, vendorId, startAt, endAt, status, notes)` + unique(vendorId, startAt)
* `Order(id, buyerId, totalNGN, feeNGN, status)`
* `OrderItem(id, orderId, type, productId?, bookingId?, qty, priceNGN)`
* `Payment(id, orderId, provider, reference, amountNGN, currencyPaid, fxRate, status)`
* `EscrowLedger(id, paymentId, vendorId, amountHeld, amountReleased, status)`
* `Fulfillment(id, orderId, method: COURIER|BIKE|WAYBILL|SELF, trackingRef, status)`
* `Dispute(id, orderId/bookingId, reason, photos, status, resolution)`
* `Review(id, vendorId, buyerId, orderId/bookingId, rating, comment)`
* `Payout(id, vendorId, amountNGN, bankRef, status)`

## 7. Non-Functional Requirements

* Mobile-first web (360px baseline), Lighthouse >85, p95 page <2.5s on 4G.
* Security: hashed passwords, rate-limit auth/OTP, webhook signature verify, RBAC, audit log for money moves.
* Reliability: idempotent webhooks (dedupe by provider reference), DB transactions for stock/slot/payment updates.
* Observability: structured logs, Sentry (post-MVP ok), admin audit trail.

---

## 8. MVP Scope

**IN (ship first):**
Single Next.js web app, email+password+OTP login, vendor KYC + admin approve, products + services CRUD, calendar slot booking with double-book guard, unified cart + Paystack checkout (card/transfer/USSD), escrow HELD/RELEASED with 72h auto-release job, manual fulfillment update + WhatsApp/SMS notify, dispute open/resolve, reviews, vendor earnings + manual payout record, admin queue.

**OUT (post-MVP):**
Native mobile apps, live GPS tracking, automated Paystack Transfers, multi-currency settlement (pay vendors in USD), in-app chat, advanced promos/coupons, courier API integration (GIG/RedStar), recommendation engine.

---

## 9. Success Metrics (first 3 months)

* 50 vendors onboarded, 200 completed orders/bookings, <5% dispute rate, >60% escrow auto-release without dispute, checkout conversion >35%, payout T+2 days.

---

## 10. Risks & Open Questions

1. Escrow licensing / holding funds: use Paystack + ledger vs licensed escrow? -> Legal review needed.
2. KYC level for payouts (BVN requirement?) -> Confirm with Paystack Transfer requirements.
3. Waybill fraud (fake receipt numbers) -> MVP requires photo of receipt + buyer confirm.
4. FX volatility for diaspora pricing -> lock quote for 30 min, expire unpaid orders.
5. Double-booking race conditions -> DB constraint + transactional slot reserve.
