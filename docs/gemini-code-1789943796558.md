# Product Requirement Document (PRD): BookNBuy

## Product Overview
BookNBuy is a two-sided, multi-vendor marketplace platform designed to bridge physical product retail with service appointments in a single unified web and mobile experience. Built specifically to handle regional market realities—such as localized logistics, informal dispatch channels, localized NGN payment ecosystems, and cross-border transactions—BookNBuy allows users to buy goods, book professional services, or list their own offerings with a single account.

---

## 1. Objectives & Goals
* **Unified Commerce:** Enable seamless cross-activity between buying goods and booking services without requiring separate account creation.
* **Trust & Security:** Reduce payment friction and fraud through automated escrow holding and a structured dispute inspection period.
* **Localized Payment & Multi-Currency Engine:** Support NGN-native payment mechanisms (virtual bank transfers, cards, USSD, mobile wallets) while allowing international diaspora buyers to pay in USD, GBP, or EUR.
* **Market-Tailored Logistics:** Accommodate formal delivery partners (couriers) as well as localized, informal delivery channels (bike dispatch, motor park waybills, and WhatsApp/SMS status updates).
* **Conversion & Retainment:** Lower cart abandonment by streamlining checkout and offering instant time-slot scheduling.

---

## 2. Target Audience & Personas

### Persona A: The Casual Buyer & Booker ("Tobi")
* **Profile:** Urban professional living in Lagos, mobile-first user.
* **Needs:** Wants to buy products (e.g., tech accessories, clothing) and book appointments (e.g., tailor, salon, home repair) from one app using instant bank transfers or debit cards.
* **Pain Points:** Unreliable fulfillment, fear of sending money before service delivery, lack of real-time communication during transit.

### Persona B: The Micro-Vendor / Service Pro ("Amaka")
* **Profile:** Runs a boutique clothing store and offers bespoke tailoring services in Abuja.
* **Needs:** A flexible dashboard to list products, control booking schedules, receive automated Naira payouts, and manage earnings.
* **Pain Points:** Double-booking issues, managing manual bank transfer proof/receipts, high operational costs with rigid logistics platforms.

### Persona C: The Diaspora Buyer ("Kemi")
* **Profile:** Nigerian living in the UK or US.
* **Needs:** Wants to pay in GBP/USD using an international card or Apple Pay to purchase products or book services for family back home in Nigeria.
* **Pain Points:** High cross-border transaction fees, merchant rejection of foreign cards, currency conversion uncertainty.
